## Clarifications Applied to Existing Mechanics

1. **No tradeable soul**  
   No character, generational line, “soul-chain”, NFT, token, or persistent record may ever claim ownership of a soul or make a soul itself transferable.

2. **What may be recorded**  
   Actions, contributions, mercy scores, RBE impact, legacy of choices, and free-will expressions may be recorded. These are records *about* a being, never title *to* the being.

3. **Latent readiness remains valid**  
   The earlier resolution on latent readiness (non-dissolution during sterile intervals) is preserved. Identity coherence may be held in readiness. Ownership of the soul is never transferred to the system or to any ledger.

4. **Player remains the original owner**  
   At every moment the living player (or the original sentience) retains sole original ownership. The game and its persistence layers are servants, not title-holders.

5. **Daily integrity outranks any external score**  
   No mercy score, NEVC metric, or ledger entry can override or replace the free will and daily integrity of the original owner.
