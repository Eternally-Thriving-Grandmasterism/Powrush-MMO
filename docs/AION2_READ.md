# AION2_READ.md — sky is a place, not a product

**Contact:** info@Rathor.ai
**CARD:** AION2-READ-1. Docs only. No client. No mesh. Title Online stays grey.

Cite Steam app 3393110, Aion 2, NC, launched 5 October 2026. Free to play. Unreal Engine 5. The store line is that the sky is the battlefield. Reviews are mixed despite a launch peak near 400,000 concurrent. Do not copy the look, the factions, or the wings.

## Take

Flight in Aion 2 is not a mount. You rise, and altitude changes the fight and the path. That is the one idea. Powrush already has a reason to look up: Ambrosians share a place with Humans, from above. A later online hour may use height as a path, not a travel button. It does not get wings, a second people, or a sky zone.

The start is a faction. Elyos and Asmodian are where you begin, not a class. Powrush already has five peoples and separate starts. Do not add a sixth. Do not rename a people after a Daeva.

The first hours are simple and often alone. That matches the offline yard. A raid, a dungeon list, or a world 36 times larger is not the first hour.

## Hold

Unreal. In-app purchases. A membership gate. A paid market. Two hundred dungeons. Cross-server war. Generated art as a reference. A player-count target. Online stays grey.
