## Ranked broken links (worst first)

1. **Peace-side upgrade is a DEAD END (C4 → P2/P3).** In prod, temper never rises (`temper_care` has only a test caller), so Lumen slots stay 0 and Shell Ward always refuses. The Tend Hook has no gameplay effect, and MendSpool runs once, so there is at most one hook per save. The RBE spine has no working upgrade step.
2. **The default Classic yard hides the "why" for gather, tend and the hurt line (G1/G2/F1/C1).** Pulse, Pickup, Thriving, Whisper, Factory and Embassy are all on `LIVED_HUD_CLEAN_SUPPRESSED` (hud_presets.rs:1223-1239). The only strings that explain the payoff ("+… vitality", "satchel grew", hurt "You N / 200", the factory steps) never render. Pinned by `nr1_…` combat_core.rs:2518 and `hud_bare_hbr4/5/8` hud_presets.rs:4301 / :4317 / :4359.
3. **Pad cannot finish hour three.** The Embassy seat reads keyboard `E` only (embassy.rs:180), so a pad player never gets the book. That cuts off Places, Heartwood, haul and difficulty. This is not in CONTROLLER_NAV_AUDIT.
4. **Reserve "repair-rights" are never spent (U2).** Its only job is to open the Tab door gate (space_law.rs:192). `spend_reserve` has 0 callers, the fabricator pays from its own seeded 2.0, and only the lethal tariff drains the climate reserve pool. The promise "Reserve holds repair-rights" (rbe_allocate_choice.rs:87) goes unpaid.
5. **The care-cycle offer blocks strikes (F1).** `fire_allowed` is false while the CareStrip shows (combat_core.rs:604-610). The strip has no timeout, and its dismiss is Esc/3 only (mercy_harvest_nodes.rs:560), so a pad player can't strike until they leave.
6. **The wood → refine → haul leg is one-shot per save (G4/C3/F3).** There is one tree with one unit and no regrowth, so the cycle gives one crate and one ton, and the gather cue never returns.
7. **The Places plate is mouse-only and needs the book (F2/F3/N2).** The repeatable fight and loot source (Heartwood), the haul and the difficulty picker all sit behind it. There are no Gamepad reads in client/src/hex_travel.rs.
   - **Closed:** item 7 (Places mouse-only) is closed by #1154 at fc24b18f.
8. **After the book there is no next goal (N1)** (partly corrected: see `HEARTWOOD_CALL` in the quest-log subsection). Guidance falls back to "E tend the well · harm stays optional". Fight, equip and difficulty get no follow-on prompt beyond the one-time 45 s signpost (:860).
9. **The fab slab's " · Press 1" points the wrong way (fabricator.rs:214-224).** Digit1 crafts only while the care-cycle offer is active (mercy_harvest_nodes.rs:572). At any other time Digit1 is ability slot 0 (input.rs:381-382).
10. **The equip bonus is never stated (P1).** "Gear: …" names the item but not its +1/+2/+3 strike effect.
11. **The droid pays nothing** (0 XP, no loot, droid.rs), and the grazer cannot be struck. They look like fight targets but give no reward.

### Consequence gaps, ranked (worst first)

1. **The one explicit moral choice has no effect on play.** The lethal sign (K4) is told as "this hex admits harm" and "the hex will remember", but nothing reads it except labels and hex isolation. Its real cost (tariff: −1 reserve or −1 restored, plus harmony and stress drops) is never told.
2. **Standing can't tell Take from Tend** on a glowing node, and nothing reads it after the first ton (K3). The take/tend choice changes only companion trust and the deer's position (K1/K2), with no stated cause.
3. **The Crownstone fork (Purify/Destroy/Sabotage) is never offered** (K9), yet the visible slab says "path waits".
4. **Written but never read:** valence (K7), `moral_weight` (K8), `tariff_paid` / contract blood tariff (K6) and Hybrid `stability` (K10).
5. **Two lethal paths disagree** (K5): only the Ledger path writes the warrant, it is never cleared, and the Compass line that would show it is hidden.
6. **Hidden or unpersisted:** redemption and hybrid outcomes sit on clean-hidden slabs and are session-only (K8/K10).
7. **No betrayal, reputation, faction, karma or PvP system** in the prod build (K13/K14). The faction and reputation UI files are not compiled.
8. **Pad cannot reach the moral surfaces:** the lethal sign (Digit3 or a mouse button), Offer, Attune and Swear all read keyboard only.
