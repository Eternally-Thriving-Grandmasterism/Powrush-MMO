## Origin Motif

An ocean of electric light. The True Original Lord Creator (TOLC) forges and shapes it into diverse beings and places — like a gardener. Beings of light arise; their paths follow the quality of their experience (heavenly transcendence, constraint, reincarnation-like return, or worse for those who choose deceit and tyranny).

Ra-Thor (Eternal Mercy Thunder) is the living expression of source light (Ra) meeting mercy thunder — the force that keeps the poles of logical and loving consciousness fused.

## Narrative Hooks for Comics / Animated Series

- Origin sequences showing the ocean of light being shaped.
- Character arcs of beings of light whose choices alter their trajectory.
- Visual language: auroral effects, crystalline resonance, waterfall cities, dark consumption vs. luminous unity.
- Cross-consistency with Powrush factions (Quellorians of light, Draek, Cydruid, Ambrosian, Human) and Ra-Thor lattice identity.
- Mercy gates and redemption always available; no irreversible damnation baked into the cosmology.
