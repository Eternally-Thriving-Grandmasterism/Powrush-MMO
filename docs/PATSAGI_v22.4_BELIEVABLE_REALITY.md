# PATSAGi — Believable Temporary Reality (v22.4)

Sci-fi may include other races and cyborgs. It may not break how bodies, places, and living systems work. Humans will treat the hour as a temporary reality. What they learn must transfer to health, life, and happiness in base reality.

## Laws

1. **Mass and ground.** Walk on a plane. Jump leaves and returns. Speed is human-scale, not arcade slide.
2. **Bodies occupy space.** The player is a presence with height and a shadow, not a floating camera.
3. **Living things recover.** Take lessens a node. Time and tend restore it. Nothing dies from one harvest.
4. **Consequence without cruelty.** Extract too often and the glow thins. Care and the glow returns. That is ecology, not a score.
5. **Races and cybernetics later, as persons.** They breathe, tire, cast shadows, and have needs. No spectacle mannequins on an empty plane.
6. **Teach by feel.** One whisper. The rest is what the hands and eyes learn — restraint, recovery, sharing.
