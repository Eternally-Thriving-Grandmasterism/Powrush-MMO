## Core Motif (In-Universe)

The universe is an ocean of electric light. The True Original Lord Creator (TOLC) forges and shapes portions of it into diverse forms — like a garden — so that beings may exist and choose. Beings of light complete the atomic circuit; their subsequent trajectory (transcendence, constraint, redemption, or return) follows the quality of their experience and choices.

## Mapping to Existing Systems

- **Quellorian / Aetherion Luminari:** Luminous ancient peoples of light and resonance. Auroral effects, crystalline ships, waterfall cities, and Unity are expressions of shaped light.
- **Ambrosian attunement & Ascension:** High-valence experience increases resonance and access to abundance paths.
- **Crownstone Trilemma & Purification:** Moral accounting and experience-based outcomes feed into Capture & Repurpose, Sabotage, or other paths.
- **RBE / Abundance:** Post-scarcity flows when harmony (shaped light) is high; scarcity appears under consumption/corruption.
- **Cydruid & Draek contrast:** Living networks vs. consumption/hivemind; redemption remains available.
