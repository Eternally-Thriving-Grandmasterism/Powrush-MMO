## Why the 2026-10-08 plates missed

The cavern plates are one teal-gold fantasy city, painted twice. They show no human cyborg, no bore-shaft, no scaffold. The orbit plates are one lava world and one crystal starfish, painted twice. Quellorian light is pale gold with violet seams, not a purple geode. Cydruid metal is teal and leaf-green trim on a machine frame, not vines on a temple. Draek colour is dry red and bronze chitin, and it does not appear.

## Shared lock

## 1. Cydruid stalactite city — powrush.com

Epic wide 16:9. An engineered city hung from stalactites in a deep cavern. The builders are humans in cyborg frames. Bore-shafts and metal scaffolds cut the stone. A glow-lake is far below and reflects warm amber lamps, not a neon temple. Trim is teal and leaf-green metal with soft gold fasteners. Instrument lights are low cyan. Stone is cool grey. No bark, no vines on the machines, no crystal spirals, no alien priests. Quiet and advanced. No text.

## 2. Fractured Earth — powrush.com

Epic wide 16:9, low orbit at dusk. Earth broken into dark rock islands with magma in the seams. Small human cities burn warm grey-gold lights on the islands. A scarred moon. Debris is thin. High above, one Quellorian mothership: radial, pale-gold hull, violet and pink light only in the seams, not a crystal starfish and not a geode. Wounded, still lived-in. No text.

## 3. Quellorian memory

Wide 16:9. A lost homeworld at two suns, one gold and one white-violet, over an ocean. Pale-gold crystalline spires with violet and pink seams. Auroral sky. No people. A memory, not a playable yard. No teal cave. No text.

## 4. Vrendak and the veterans

16:9. Vrendak the Unbound at a rough camp on the fractured-Earth fringe. Tall humanoid, dry red and bronze chitin, matte black plate, dull gold circuitry, empty dark brow socket. Not a reptile. A few unnamed Draek veterans behind him, same materials, disciplined. Magma islands far off. Ember, crimson, twilight blue. No teal temple. No text.

## 5. Five leaders — powrush.com

16:9 lineup, same painterly family, fractured-Earth sky behind them. Five bodies, five materials:

1. Orin Hollowell: human-proportioned full cyborg frame, teal and leaf-green metal trim, gold fasteners, cyan instrument lights. No bark, no vines.
2. Solmaris Orynthe: tall slender alien man, pale-gold crystalline plates, violet and pink seams, faint runes.
3. Lyrisse: partly translucent crystalline woman, inner lattice, prism white and gold, faint rainbow hem. Not a copy of Solmaris.
4. Ronan Ashcroft: human man, warm grey-gold field plate, scarred, tools, no glow.
5. Vrendak: dry red and bronze chitin, matte black plate, empty brow socket. Not a reptile.

No text.

## 6. Emblems

16:9 sheet, five marks on worn gear, no letters.

1. Cydruid: stalactite over a glow-lake, etched in teal-trimmed metal.
2. Quellorian: two suns in a pale-gold crystal star, violet enamel.
3. Ambrosian: a prism splitting one line of light, on clear crystal.
4. Human: a tower on a broken island, painted on grey-gold plate.
5. Unbound: a cracked crown, stamped in matte black and red chitin.
