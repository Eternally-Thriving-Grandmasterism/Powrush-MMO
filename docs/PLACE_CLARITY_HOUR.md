## 0. What clarity means here

One question: **can a stranger name the room they are standing in before they read a slab?**

Clarity is spent on that sentence and nothing else. It is not a new room, not a new verb, not a new plate, not a new asset. Everything below is a **reading** of what is already on main (`README` Agent landing, walked U0–U8 and the stamps listed there) — it names the read the hour owes, and it names what stays parked.

Acceptance keeps the `RBE_FIRST_HOUR` shape: a stranger who read no docs can still say *"if I only take, the glow fades"* — and, after this clarity, also *"that was the wood room, that was the pipe room."*

## 1. Four rooms = four bodies

`FUN_WITHOUT_WOW` §1.3 names the rooms as **four save-slots on disk**; §2.1 gives each room **one material**; `SIM_AND_HAND_CANON` §2 gives each room **one muscle**. This section is the Hour-facing join of those sentences: one room, one material-read, one body the hands take on.

**Body** = how the stranger stands, and what the same Peace **E** feels like in that room. Same verb everywhere (`INPUT_CANON` — Use is always **E** / pad South). Different body.

| Place | One material-read | Body the hour teaches | First-seconds read (no slab) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanctuary** | Yard ground — the teaching well | **Standing.** Boot yard; Play always boots here (`OFFLINE_SKU`). Walk, tend, satchel, allocate. | Open yard, wells, climate fog. Nothing overhead, nothing edged. |
| **Heartwood** | Wood — living lamp and rim | **Living.** Peace room; lamp disk stays empty and Lip dress stays on Heartwood (`PLACES_BIBLE`). | Wood rim and hanging ribs around a quiet centre; the pond drop is a **bath**, not a death. |
| **Threshold** | Pipe — look and tend | **Leaning in.** Pipe **E** is **Tend**, not harvest (`d7bcb61c`). May share Heartwood's file. | One shelf and one pipe; a door-shaped room you look through, not a lobby you queue in. |
| **Depths** | Wet stone — one landing, restore | **Descending.** One landing; own hex file; **E** restores and is not Take. | Wet stone underfoot, one way down, one way home. Quiet that remembers extraction. |

Read of the table:

- **Four rooms and only four.** Not five Sanctuary clones. No Heartwood / Depths spawned on Prime. Sanctuary stays **yard only** (`PLACES_BIBLE`, `OFFLINE_SKU`).
- **One material each — never two.** A room that needs two materials to be legible is a failed read, not an argument for a fifth room.
- **Material is presentation, not sim truth.** Material, fog paint, and bed audio are outputs of \(F\); the verb and the week counters are \(F\) (`SIM_AND_HAND_CANON` §4). Clarity may never move a number into the render path.
- **The body does not change the hand.** WASD · look · one **E** on the local FixedUpdate tick, one Use buffer ≤120 ms, stop-on-release (`FUN_WITHOUT_WOW` §1.1). Four bodies, one hand.
- **Rooms arrive in the hour's order, not a menu's.** Esc → Places opens after **Settled + book**; the four rows are hexes on disk, not overlays on Prime.

## 2. Well · Grove · Ember — readable dress, not a new system

The three names are **already named canon** and stay where canon put them: the **Heartwood Wards** seal posts, placed outside the lamp disk and outside the water (`PLACES_BIBLE` ring 3 · Wards; walked U8 in `README` / `SLICE_LOG`). They are the same three words as the cosmetic house seals in `LAUNCH_UX` S2 / D3 — strings, not stats.

What they mean **in the hour**:

| Seal | Read in the hour | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Well** | The taking the House has answered for — the yard's teaching well, said as a post you can stand at. | Not a stock, not a spawner, not a second ledger. |
| **Grove** | The living side of the wood room — what came back. Shares its word with the opt-in light-gen setting; the post is dress either way. | Not a gate on `Grove · light`, not a scatter claim, not a biome unlock. |
| **Ember** | Warmth carried, not lethal — the quiet end of the wood room. | Not a fire mechanic, not a harm row, not a lethal sign. |

Standing law this file does **not** move:

- **Dress only.** Look and tend on the posts change **session-local** dress. They **do not write the house book** (`PLACES_BIBLE` ring 3; walked U8 slice line).
- **Heartwood-local.** The posts sit on Heartwood, outside the lamp disk and the bath. Not on Sanctuary. Not a **Places row**. Not Market. Not a save slot.
- **Cosmetic strings.** Three words plus the optional heritage caption, string only. **No combat stats** on seals or recipes — no +STR / +take / DPS (`PLACES_BIBLE` · Recipes).
- **One notice, one verb.** The near-post line stays the single Wards notice on the one HUD; **E** there is the same Peace Use, spent as tend-dress.
- **Skippable.** The seals were skippable at the House step and stay skippable. A stranger who never reads them still finishes the hour.

Clarity intent, stated as intent only: the three words should land as **three readable posts in the wood room**, named without a tutorial line. That is a legibility ask against dress that already exists — not a new system, not a new asset, and not a code claim from this file.

## 3. H still hides — one HUD, and the hush stays

**H wins.** Hide guidance stays **H** on keyboard (`INPUT_CANON`), and the hush it buys is the point: a stranger may turn the teaching down and keep playing.

What this file re-states and does not widen:

- **One HUD.** Satchel (**I**), ledger sash (**L**), climate slab, one card. That is the chrome. **No second HUD**, no F-row, no XP bar, no peer count, no talent panel, no Living Practice overlay (`FUN_WITHOUT_WOW` §2.3, `STRANGER_LOOP` Quiet law, `INPUT_CANON` refuse list).
- **The sentence survives the hush.** With guidance hidden, the well still speaks its state — Idle · Glowing · Tended · Resting · Stressed — as a short well line with its token. Hiding guidance hides **guidance**, not the world's answer (audited **PASS**, `docs/PLAYTEST_AUDIT_2026-09-09.md` row 2).
- **A hideable line is a line, not a second surface.** Anything clarity adds for a room read must be **hideable by the same H** and must live on the one HUD. If a read needs its own toggle or its own panel, the read is wrong — fix the material, not the chrome.
- **H is a session toggle over a persisted default.** Settings *Hide slabs* is the saved default; **H** still toggles inside the session. No second hide row, no second mute row, no F-row.
- **The hush is audio too.** Bed / sting / lamp hush are silenced by the existing **Mute**, and unmute never opens a socket (`FUN_WITHOUT_WOW` §2.2).

The test for any later clarity change, in one line: **press H, and the room must still be nameable.** If a room only read because a caption was on screen, the material did not do its job.
