## 2. Player promise (one paragraph bots may quote)

A stranger boots Offline. They walk a yard. A well glows. They press **E** and the world answers *this tick*. They put the take in a satchel (**I**) and choose **Flow** or **Reserve** (**R**). If they only take, the glow fades. If they give back, the climate returns. They quit. They return. The yard remembers. That is the game. Friendship and embassy come after the House is Settled — and Online stays grey until the steward writes `online yes`.

If a slice does not make that paragraph truer, do not ship it.

## 4. Wholesomeness without sermon

Feel-good media works because the world is kind *and* specific. Powrush already has the grammar:

- Origin of a resource is **observation**, never ownership of a soul (`LICENSE`, README).
- Week score is **tons + restored**, not kills, not XP (`FUN_WITHOUT_WOW`).
- Threshold **E** is Tend, not harvest. Depths **E** is restore, not Take.
- Lethal is opt-in Ledger **3**, never default E.
- Welcome slab: *Welcome back · Hour two held · the yard remembers.*

**Do this (feel):**
1. Make well speech *audible and visible as words* (Idle / Glowing / Tended / Resting / Stressed). Colourblind tokens. Mute kills bed + sting.
2. Make allocate **consequential in the yard this session** — Flow visibly helps a Resting neighbor; Reserve visibly banks a repair-right the player can spend on a tired node later. If allocate is a menu that changes a number and nothing in the climate, it is busywork. Fix that before any new Place.
3. Keep House naming skippable. *Unnamed House* is valid belonging.
4. After Settled, Places are four rooms with **one material each** (yard / wood / pipe / wet stone). A stranger should name the room by what it is made of.

**Do not do (sermon):**
- Divine Whisper walls in the first ten minutes.
- 11-language onboarding before the first E.
- Epiphany card with a TOLC essay.
- “You are a better person now” toast.
- Council vote UI in hour one.

Wholesomeness is the well recovering because you allocated Flow. That is the movie.

## 7. Challenge that is not busywork

Wise challenge in this SKU:

1. **Climate** — take vs restore, readable in L as two lines: *this week* vs **House week**.
2. **Place muscle** — yard teaches take/tend; pipe teaches look/tend; depths teach restore. Switching rooms is the difficulty curve, not a talent tree.
3. **Charter door** — Tab / Q / L after allocate. Peace visitor on Frontier: E is *Not your charter*. That sentence is the social lesson before multiplayer exists.
4. **Opt-in lethal** — Ledger 3. Bind stays default. Blood tariff is a moral cost, not a DPS rotation.

Refuse: XP bar, daily quests, durability busywork, fetch-ten-glow, empty crafting queues, second HUD, F-row.

If a task can be skipped with no change to the climate or the House bill, it is chore. Cut it.
