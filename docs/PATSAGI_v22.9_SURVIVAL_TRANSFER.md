# PATSAGi — Survival Transfer (v22.9)

ARK is useful because it makes time, weather, and other lives *matter*. It is not a template for hunger death or knockout taming.

## Keep

- **Cycles.** What you carry wants to move. Use it, share it, or it goes home to the grove.
- **Hands.** Tend more in a climate and the place answers faster. That is skill, not a level number.
- **Other lives.** A companion approaches care and may carry you when trust is real.
- **Climate.** Depths keep freshness longer. Horizon lets it go sooner. Adapt.

## Refuse

- Starvation drain as a teacher.
- Narcotic / knockout tame.
- Creatures as inventory fridges.
- Fake tribes while we are still one human on one machine.

## Transfer to base reality

Eat what is fresh. Share what you cannot use. Living things are not bags. Trust is built by care, not by force.
