## 2. Short scorecard for a human walk

| Beat | Ask after play | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| **Wonder** | “What made you move?” | The player names a world cue, not a menu obligation. |
| **Hand** | “What did E do here?” | The player distinguishes care / take / restore in the Place they stood. |
| **Climate** | “What happens if you only take?” | The player expects the glow to tire or stress. |
| **Return** | “How do you answer it?” | The player names Flow, Reserve-for-Mend, or Tend without saying sell. |
| **Week** | “What is the score?” | The player says tons + restored and distinguishes current hex from House sum. |
| **Place** | “Where were you?” | With **H** pressed, the room remains nameable by material / body and the surviving Place + mood answer. |
| **Myth boundary** | “Is the Codex still in the playable yard?” | The player says no: it foreshadows; it does not prove a shipped mesh, Online world, or gameplay scene. |

Do not turn this into telemetry theatre. A human note with a direct quote and the observed beat is stronger than a dashboard full of ungrounded engagement numbers.
