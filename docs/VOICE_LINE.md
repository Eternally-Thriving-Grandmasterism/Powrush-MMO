# VOICE_LINE.md — a line when the hour earns it

**Contact:** info@Rathor.ai
**CARD:** VOICE-LINE-1. Docs only. No client. No mesh. Title Online stays grey.

Cite Heroes of Newerth announcer packs. Those fire a fixed phrase on a kill or a win. Powrush does not copy that pack. A line speaks when the hour has earned it.

## Take

A voice line is a short line tied to a people and a situation. The five peoples already have voice direction in docs/PLAYABLE_RACES.md. A line may fire on a first tend, a stressed well, a door, or a rare generation. It does not fire on every step. It does not taunt. It does not announce a kill.

Procedural means a seed plus a short line, generated offline from the people's tone. The player may mark the seed as a keepsake, per docs/KEEPSAKE_SEED.md. The mark does not pay. The line is not a model speaking every frame.

## Hold

A constant announcer. A Duke-style taunt pack. Generated speech that leaves the machine. Online stays grey. The yard stays quiet until the hour earns a line.
